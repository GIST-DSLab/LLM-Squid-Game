## How words were counted
- I read line boxes from `pdftotext -bbox-layout`, restored reading order column by column (split at x = 306), and cut figure, table and equation regions by their y coordinates.
- Excluded: title block, abstract (71 words) and keywords (11), Figure 1 (top of p.1 right column) with its caption, the unnumbered town map in the p.3 left column, Figure 2 (full width, bottom of p.3), Figure 3 (full width, top half of p.4), Figure 4 (full width, top of p.5), Table 1 with its caption (p.5 left column), three display equations (p.2: the piecewise p(ask), the product likelihood, V(u)), page numbers, the CC BY footer, Acknowledgements and References (~895 words).
- Included: inline math in sentences and the bullet list in "Alternate models". Any token with a letter or digit counts as one word. Sub/superscripts with no gap (mBob, e1) were merged into one token, matching plain pdftotext. Heading and run-in heading words were not counted.
- Body total = 3,753. The level-1 shares sum to 1.000 after rounding, with no adjustment.

## Heading levels (checked on rendered pages)
- Level 1 (centred bold): Introduction, Computational framework, Instantiating the framework to explain surprising plans (two lines), Discussion, Conclusion. Acknowledgements and References follow on p.7.
- Level 2 (left bold): Inferring Bob's erroneous mental model and Correcting Bob's mental model under Computational framework. Computational model, Alternate models, Experiment and Results under Instantiating. Three subsections under Discussion.
- Level 3 (bold run-in): "Effect of the social cost", "Effect of reasoning about the future" and "Effect of reasoning about the past" under Results.
- pdftotext -layout makes "Experiment" and "Results" look like top-level headings. The rendered page shows they are left-aligned level-2 headings in the same style as "Alternate models". So the paper has no top-level experiment section.
- Untitled lead-ins: Computational framework (208 words before its first subheading), Instantiating (211 words describing the town, the four exceptional conditions and the example scenario), and Results (108 words before the run-ins). Each appears as an "(untitled lead-in ...)" child, so the children sum to the parent.

## Anything odd
- Role of "Instantiating the framework ..." is set to "result". It is the single study section, but it also holds the domain-specific model (Computational model + Alternate models, 419 words, about a quarter of the section). The general model has its own level-1 section (role "method").
- Experiment has no Method/Results split under its own heading. The procedure is written as plain text under "Experiment", and "Results" is its sibling, not its child.
- No footnotes, no equation numbers, no code, data or preregistration links.
- The Discussion has no summary paragraph. It goes straight into three subsections; the last one (an applied JavaScript-explanation tool from concurrent work) is the longest at 344 words.
