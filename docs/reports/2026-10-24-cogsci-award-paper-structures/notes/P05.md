# P05 — extraction notes

The eScholarship item 43h3z261 is abstract-only: a 2-page PDF made of the cover plus one page with the abstract, proceedings p.1661. The structure was therefore taken from the author-hosted full paper (https://www.danielhorschler.com/docs/RoykaEtAl-2024-ToMErrors.pdf, 8 pages, CogSci 2-column format, no cover; Amanda Royka's publications page also links a PDF). The two abstracts differ. The author-PDF version, used in the JSON, is longer: it adds "(n=419 subjects)", "to test the circumstances under which…" and "which we argue may also be shared with human infants". The eScholarship version is shorter and says "perform inconsistently". The OSF link is an anonymized view-only link, so this PDF may be the submission or camera-ready manuscript rather than a proceedings-typeset copy. Heading levels: level 1 is 12pt bold centred (Introduction, Study 1–3, General Discussion). Level 2 is 11pt bold flush-left (Methods, Results and Discussion). Level 3 is a 10pt bold run-in (Subjects, Apparatus, Procedure, Video Coding, with no trailing period). "OSF Repository" is a bold run-in label on its own line at the end of the General Discussion; it is recorded as a level-3 child of General Discussion, not as a section heading. Study 3 has an 81-word rationale paragraph before its Methods; Studies 1 and 2 begin directly with Methods. Study 2 has no Apparatus subsection; it points back to Study 1. Word counts leave out the Figure 1 caption, which is unusually long (about 140 words, split across several PyMuPDF blocks that were removed by hand), the Fig. 2 caption, Table 1 (7pt) and footnotes. Many justified lines were split into single-word fragments by the extractor, but every fragment was still counted. Paragraph counts (Intro 6, General Discussion about 7) are approximate, because the first paragraph after a heading is not indented. There are no Acknowledgments. References take all of p.7 plus the top quarter of the left column of p.8.
