Layout (cover excluded): p.1-4 body; p.5 is entirely Figure 2 (six example scenarios + caption), with no body text; p.6 has Table 1 and Figure 3 stacked full-width across the top (~40% of the text area), then the end of Results, Discussion, and Acknowledgements in the right column. Word counts: `pdftotext` per page with the two columns cropped separately (x 0-306 / 306-612), then line ranges picked by hand. Excluded: title block, abstract (201) and keywords (9), all captions, text inside figures, Table 1, display equations (numbered Eq. 1-8, the unnumbered PDDL grammar `φ ::= ...`, the weight-update rule and the expectation formula), the "Equal Contribution" footnote, page numbers, the CC BY footer, Acknowledgements (68) and references (~690). Inline math inside sentences was kept: every token with a letter or digit counts as one word (same rule as P11), so "Joint Inference" and "Quantitatively Interpreting" are slightly inflated by symbols. Heading levels (checked on rendered pages): level 1 centred bold (Introduction, Computational Model, Experiments, Results, Discussion, then Acknowledgements and References); level 2 left bold; level 3 = bold run-in baseline names under "Alternative Models" (Omniscient Observer, Ignorant Observer, (Uncertain) Non-Mentalizing Observer, Heuristic Mentalizer). Computational Model and Experiments each open with an untitled lead-in paragraph, recorded as an "(untitled lead-in ...)" child so children sum to the parent.
