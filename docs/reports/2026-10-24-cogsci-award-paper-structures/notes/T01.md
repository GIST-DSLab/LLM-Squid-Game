## Heading levels (checked visually)
- Level 1 (centred, bold, larger): "Part I: Eliciting language about object structure" (wraps onto two lines) and "Part II: Identifying concepts from language".
- Level 2 (left, bold): Methods and Results under each Part.
- Level 3 (bold, run-in): Participants, Stimuli, Task procedure, Language preprocessing; the two Results claims in Part I; the two Modeling paragraphs and two Results claims in Part II.
- **Odd:** "Discussion" uses the level-2 style and comes straight after Part II's Results. Its content covers the whole paper ("In this paper, we look to natural language..."), so I list it as a level-1 section with role "disc". If you follow the typography strictly, it would be a third level-2 child of Part II. In that case Part II would be 1,803 words (0.498).
- Role labels: both Parts are marked "result". Each one has its own Methods and Results. Part II is the modelling part, but it reports model results, so it is not a pure "method" section. The paper has no separate Model section.
- Each Part has an untitled lead paragraph before its Methods heading (41 and 209 words). These are entered as "(lead paragraph)" children, so the children add up to the parent.

## How words were counted
- I used `pdftotext -bbox-layout` and took line coordinates. Each page was split into columns at x = 306 and cut by y range, so the reading order is left column then right column.
- Excluded: the title block; the abstract and keywords; the regions of all four figures together with their captions (this includes the word lists inside Fig. 2B, the program code in Fig. 3 and the axis labels in Fig. 4); the display equation Eq. 1 (PMI, p.3); footnote 1 (p.4) and footnote 2 (p.5); page numbers and the p.1 proceedings footer; heading-only lines; the Acknowledgments; the code-link box; the references.
- Included: run-in level-3 heading words, counted with their paragraphs (same as P07); inline math tokens such as "L i", "π", and C_L = |L| + (1/N)Σ|π_L|. Any token with a letter or digit counts as one word, and a line-break hyphen counts as two words (same as P09). Subscripted symbols split into extra tokens, so Part II is inflated slightly, probably by about 20–30 words.
- Body total = 3,620. Shares: Intro 0.167, Part I 0.335, Part II 0.339, Discussion 0.159. Part II's raw share of 0.3395 was rounded down so the four shares sum to 1.000.

## Other notes
- The paper has one data-collection study (Part I). Part II fits candidate program libraries to the same descriptions. It uses IBM Model 1 translation probabilities and held-out mean per-word log-likelihood.
- The Part I/II framing is announced at the end of the Introduction ("In Part I, ... In Part II, ..."), and every Results run-in heading states its finding as a full sentence.
- There is no Related Work section. Prior work appears in the Introduction and in the fourth Discussion paragraph.
