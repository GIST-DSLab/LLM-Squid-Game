# P08 notes (Chandra, Li, Tenenbaum & Ragan-Kelley, CogSci 2023)

Title mismatch: the CogSci 2023 award listing names the Marr Prize paper "Acting as Inverse Inverse Planning". That is the SIGGRAPH 2023 title (arXiv 2305.16913, ACM DOI 10.1145/3588432.3591510). The only CogSci 2023 proceedings paper by these authors is "Storytelling as Inverse Inverse Planning" (eScholarship qt3cx919kp, pp. 903-912), and it says it "reports on work to be presented at SIGGRAPH 2023". I analysed this CogSci version, not the SIGGRAPH/arXiv version. Plain `pdftotext` scrambles the reading order here: column 2 of p.1 comes out before the abstract, and the figure text is interleaved. So I extracted each page column by column (`pdftotext -x/-y/-W/-H`) and counted from that file. The full-width figures were split across the two column crops, and I dropped their fragments by line range. Equations were left in the word counts (a small inflation, about 50 words). Body total = 4,181 words, not counting the abstract (215), acknowledgements (84) or references (~1,480 across pp.7-10). Heading levels were checked visually on 70-dpi renders:
- level 1, centred bold: Introduction, Background: Bayesian inverse planning, Inverse inverse planning, Discussion.
- level 2, left bold: Behavioral experiment 1, Miming by "inverse inverse physics", Behavioral experiment 2, Flexibility of inverse inverse planning (all videos).
- level 3, bold run-in with a "(video)" hyperlink: Plot twists, Irony, Flashbacks, Narrative arc, Stumbling.

"Miming" is a level-2 sibling of the experiments, not a new level-1 section, so the physics demo and its experiment are nested under the method section. The 463 words between the "Inverse inverse planning" heading and "Behavioral experiment 1" are listed as an untitled lead child. Unlike the 2024 papers, this paper uses 6 content pages plus about 3.1 pages of references; the CogSci 2023 template allowed unlimited reference pages. The references are long because nearly every entry has a "Retrieved from" URL. The abstract is unusual. It reads like a mini-introduction ("Great storytelling takes us on a journey…") and ends with a pointer to the SIGGRAPH paper; it has no numeric results. The headline numbers (12x more effective, 5x heavier) appear only at the end of the Introduction.
