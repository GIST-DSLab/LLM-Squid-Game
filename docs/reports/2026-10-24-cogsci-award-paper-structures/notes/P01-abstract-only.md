# P01 notes (Braswell et al., Wild Possibilities, CogSci 2025 Marr Prize)

The CogSci 2025 proceedings hold this paper as **abstract-only**. https://escholarship.org/content/qt2n33k1ks/qt2n33k1ks.pdf has 2 pages: the eScholarship cover and one page (proceedings p.1447) with the title, four authors (one per line, each with an affiliation line), the 149-word abstract and the CC-BY/editor footer. The journalpub.escholarship.org galley (article 49326/galley 37287) is the same one-page abstract. Web searches found no arXiv, PsyArXiv, bioRxiv, OSF or lab-site copy of the full 6-page paper, and caplab.yale.edu did not resolve. For that reason the heading outline, word shares, figures and tables are empty, and `pages_body` = 1 counts only the abstract page. The only structural signal is the abstract's sentence order: framing, gap, test case, prior failures, four-experiment result, reinterpretation, implication. A related full paper by the same group, on 3-year-old children (Meade, Braswell, Santos, Cesana-Arlotti, Rossano), is at https://escholarship.org/uc/item/63f5h72v, but that is a different paper and was not analysed. To use a 6-page Marr-Prize structure, replace P01 with another award paper, or ask the authors for the full manuscript.
