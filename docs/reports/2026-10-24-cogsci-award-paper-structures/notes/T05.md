# T05 notes (Cheyette, Chen, Hofer, Callaway, Bramley & Tenenbaum, CogSci 2025)

Source: eScholarship proceedings copy (qt517147vr, vol. 47, pp. 4754-4761). The PDF has 9 pages: the cover plus 8 manuscript pages. The cover is excluded from page numbering. The CogSci 2025 society page lists the paper winners (Lu; Zhu), and this paper is not among them, so it is recorded as a presented paper with no award.

Page layout (cover excluded): the body runs from p.1 to p.6 and ends at the bottom of the p.6 right column with "Discussion and Future Work". References fill p.7 and about a quarter of the p.8 left column. There are no Acknowledgments, appendix or footnotes, only the conference CC BY footer on p.1.

Word counts: I ran `pdftotext` separately on the left and right column of each page (x 0-306 / 306-612) to restore reading order. I joined words split by a hyphen at a line break, then removed these by hand: the title block, the abstract (154 words) and keywords (11), all text inside figures, the captions of Figures 1-6, the content and caption of Table 1, the two unnumbered display equations (p_L in the p.3 left column, Q(a_c|s) in the p.3 right column), page numbers and the footer. Inline math (e.g. the likelihood L(θ) in Exp. 2 Results) is counted, with any token holding a letter or digit counted as one word. Heading words are not counted. References come to about 832 words. The body total is 3,675 words.

Heading levels were checked visually. Level 1 is centred bold: Introduction, Model, Experiments and Results, Discussion and Future Work. Level 2 is left bold: "Experiment 1: Guided Subproblem Solving" and "Experiment 2: Self-directed Subproblem Selection". Level 3 is bold run-in: Bounded Reasoner and Metacognitive Planner under Model; Stimuli, Procedure and Results under Exp. 1; Procedure and Results under Exp. 2 (it reuses the Exp. 1 boards, so it has no Stimuli).
