# P03 notes (Rathi, Futrell, Jurafsky, Soft production preferences, CogSci 2025)

Method: the same PyMuPDF pipeline as P02. Level 1 = 12-pt bold centered, level 2 = 11-pt bold left. There are no run-in level-3 headings. Bold 10-pt phrases such as "soft production preferences", "predictive information", "entropy rate", "first order preference" and "short-before-long preference" are bold term definitions inside sentences, not headings. Equations, the numbered linguistic examples (1)-(3) and the monospace toy-language listings in the Simulation Setup subsections (Inconsolata font) are counted as body words, so the Model and Setup counts are slightly inflated (by about 30-60 words each). Table 1's cell text (10-pt body font) was removed by hand: 13 words from Conclusion and 24 from Acknowledgements, where the column-ordering pass had placed them. Footnotes (9-pt, four of them) are excluded. Note that the user brief lists the affiliation as Stanford, but Richard Futrell is listed at UC Irvine. In plain `pdftotext`, Study 4's Results paragraph appears after the Conclusion text because of the column layout. Font-based reading order places it correctly in the left column of p.6 under "Results". The JSON adds `words_incl_children`, `pages_text_before_references` (=6) and per-child word counts. All Study sections are siblings at level 1 with an identical "Simulation Setup / Results" pair, except Study 4: "Model Prediction / Corpus Preparation / Results".
