# P07 notes (Wilschut, Sense & van Rijn, CogSci 2024)

PDF is the eScholarship proceedings copy (qt57w2x9vr, pp. 572-578; the cover page was excluded). Do not confuse it with the 2025 Topics in Cognitive Science journal version, which has the same title and turns up first in web searches. Body word counts come from plain `pdftotext` output. I removed by hand the Figure 1 panel labels, Table 1, Table 2 (its numbers spill into the Results text in pdftotext output, about 130 lines), all captions, both footnotes, the page numbers and the copyright footer. Run-in level-3 headings (Learning blocks, Test, Questionnaire) are counted together with their paragraphs. Body total = 3,716 words, not counting the abstract (154) or references (~845). Heading levels were checked visually: level 1 is centred bold, level 2 is left bold, level 3 is bold run-in. The "Results" heading has a footnote marker ("Results¹"), and that footnote holds the OSF link. This is the only open-materials link, and it sits under Results rather than in Methods. Figure 4 spans the full width but its two plots fill only about half of it. Table 1 is small and sits in the right column of p.2, next to "Learning blocks", even though Participants is where it is cited. The paper won the Computational Modeling Prize for Applied Cognition, yet its "model" is only a 208-word verbal description of an existing ACT-R spacing algorithm (Sense et al., 2016) inside Methods, with no equations and no fitting. The modelling contribution is that the model's speed-of-forgetting parameter is used as a dependent measure (M3).
