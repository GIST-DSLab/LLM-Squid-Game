# P02 notes (Suresh, Mukherjee et al., AI-enhanced semantic feature norms, CogSci 2025)

Method: PyMuPDF font-level parsing, reading order = left column then right column per page, with full-width blocks placed by vertical position. Words were counted only in 10-pt body fonts (Nimbus Roman + math/mono fonts), and blocks starting with "Figure N:" were excluded. Figure-internal text is in Helvetica/Arial, so it is excluded automatically. The 9-pt abstract and the 9-pt footnote are excluded from body counts. In-text heading words were subtracted from run-in paragraphs. The paper uses four heading styles: 12-pt bold centered (level 1), 11-pt bold "Methods" (level 2; it appears only once, inside Study I, and Study I has no matching level-2 "Results"), 10-pt bold run-in (level 3, including "Results." and "Using Flan T5 and GPT-4o to impute the AI-enhanced matrix."), and italic run-in paragraph labels (recorded as level 4 with `"style": "italic run-in"`, e.g. Concept selection./Feature elicitation./Participants./Stimuli and procedure.). Some italic labels end without a period ("Participants 31 participants…" in Study 2). The level hierarchy is visual rather than logical: the first four italic paragraphs of Study I sit directly under the level-1 heading, before "Methods". One bold "786" inside a sentence and NOVA letters in the abstract are emphasis, not headings. The JSON adds three keys beyond the template: `words_incl_children` per level-1 section (`share` is computed from this), `pages_text_before_references` (=6), and `style` on children. References are 10-pt here (not the usual 9-pt), which makes the reference list look longer (~1.5 pages). The monospace prompt block on p.4 is counted as body words (~40 words). Typos in the original ("densiry", "jdugments") were left as is.
