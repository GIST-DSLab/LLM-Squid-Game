**What the counts mean.**
- An L1's `words` covers its whole subtree. The text before the first L2 is 139 words in "Persistent Disagreement", 254 in Experiment 1 and 132 in Experiment 2.
- L3 run-in counts run from that run-in heading to the next one in reading order, so they add up to their L2.
- The quoted participant prompt in Exp. 1 Methods is counted as body text.
- Experiment 1's Results L2 heading is on p.3, though its Methods L2 starts on p.2.
- Both experiments repeat the same run-in labels: Participants, Materials and Procedure, Perceived Consensus & Persistence, Explanations for Persistence, Cross-domain Variation.
