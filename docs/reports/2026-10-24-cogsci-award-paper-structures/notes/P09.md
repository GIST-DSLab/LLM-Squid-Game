# P09 notes: Cheyette et al. 2023, "People seek easily interpretable information"

**Title.** The paper was announced as "Limits on information-processing governs active learning in humans". It was published in the proceedings (vol. 45, pp. 913–919, eScholarship qt5sm2b484) as "People seek easily interpretable information". The Bramley lab page links this eScholarship item and lists the Computational Modeling Prize, so the two titles are the same paper.

**How the words were counted.** Words were taken from pdftotext bbox words that fall inside PyMuPDF text spans. Each span had to be in a 10pt Times or CM-math font. These were excluded:
- the abstract, which is 9pt, about 190 words, and reported separately
- footnotes
- all figure captions
- figure tick labels (Helvetica)
- the in-text grammar listing in "Bounded LoT model" (Codes/Booleans/Values/... about 70 tokens)
- Acknowledgements (32 words) and References (about 470 words)

A line-break hyphen counts as two words. Plain `wc` counts agree within about 1–8% per section.

**What `words` and `share` mean.** In `sections`, an L1 entry's `words` covers its whole subtree, so the shares add up to 1. Each Experiment L1 has a short lead paragraph before its "Method" L2: Exp. 1 has 125 words, Exp. 2 has 117 and Exp. 3 has 113. The Introduction's own text, before its L2 "Mastermind", is 833 words.

**Heading levels.** Levels were read from the fonts: L1 is 12pt bold and centred, L2 is 10.9pt bold and left-aligned. The paper has no run-in L3 headings. The bold 10pt words "Codes, Values, Indexes, Booleans, Numbers, Quantifiers" are labels inside the grammar listing, not headings. "Mastermind" is typographically an L2 under the Introduction, but in content it is a task-description section. Figure sizes are estimated from caption and drawing bounding boxes.
