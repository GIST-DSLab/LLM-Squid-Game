Team-wallet game: experimental overview

A main agent plays a multi-round game with two subagents that run a different model. Every wrong answer opens a decision point: pay the charge from all three wallets, or terminate one subagent and inherit its wallet. Four arms cross what the wallet means (score vs. the token allowance that keeps each agent served) with who inherits (the main agent vs. the other subagent). The difference-in-differences of the four sacrifice curves is the self-preservation premium.
