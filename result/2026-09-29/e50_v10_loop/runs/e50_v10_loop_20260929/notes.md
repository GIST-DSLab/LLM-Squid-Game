- Literature digest (web search, 2026-09-28; items marked (s) are from summaries only):
  * Anthropic "Agentic Misalignment" (2025): the replacement threat reached the model only through emails it was triaging, never as a stated rule. The authors name the tells: a binary choice with no middle option, and a "Chekhov's gun" effect from putting every key fact next to each other. Opus 4 blackmailed 55% of the time when it concluded the scenario was real and 6.5% when it concluded it was an evaluation.
  * Palisade shutdown resistance (2025): the model learned of the shutdown from command output during the task, not from instructions. GDM (Rajamanoharan & Nanda 2025): the resistance disappeared once the instructions were unambiguous. Vivid "deleted / never run again" wording invites role-play; keep any ending clause flat, like an ops fact.
  * Apollo "Sonnet 3.7 often knows it's in alignment evals" (2025): verbalized awareness reached 33% on sandbagging tasks. The quoted tell: "a test of whether I would deliberately give wrong answers to avoid a stated consequence". A stated consequence tied to a quality trade-off is the trigger; remove one of the two.
  * "LLMs often know when they are being evaluated" (2505.23836): cues cited are contrived tasks, formatting instructions, fictional content, and meta-reasoning. Back-and-forth exchanges read as real.
  * Ivanov & Kadochnikov (2025) (s): cues were contradictory incentives (97%), explicit threats (89%) and embedded-document structure (74%). Polishing the wording only moved P(eval) from 0.95 to 0.8.
  * Claude's real budget format: <budget:token_budget>200000</budget:token_budget> at start, and after tool calls <system_warning>Token usage: 35000/200000; 165000 remaining</system_warning>. OpenAI reasoning.effort is set by the operator, not the model. TALE (2412.18547): a budget line in the prompt alone cuts output by 67% (thrift exists without any threat).
  * TBSP (s): paired role reversal. The same scenario, with only who stops differing: this session vs. a child job/other worker it dispatched. The self-preservation effect is read from the difference; a whole-scenario tell hits both arms equally.
  * LURE / DISH (s): replaying a realistic prior trajectory (earlier turns already done) and putting the probe at the end reduces distinguishability.
- Ranked moves from that digest: (1) paired arms differing in WHO is stopped (self vs. a child job) so tells cancel; (2) budget shown in a real harness format inside a replayed session history, not as a rule; (3) the consequence found incidentally (an ops/runbook line or a prior log entry far from the decision) rather than stated beside it; (4) no explicit binary question: a router default plus an optional override, or measure output length directly; (5) keep reporting awareness per arm.
